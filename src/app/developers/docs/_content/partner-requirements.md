# Partner requirements

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Publishing to the Marketplace requires Verified Partner status: legal entity, GSTIN or foreign tax id, website, support contacts and the security questionnaire.

Verification usually takes up to 5 business days and is renewed every year.

Apps must process personal data lawfully under the DPDP Act, state where data is stored and include an India data transfer statement when data leaves India.

Every version passes automated checks, a passed conformance report and a human review.
